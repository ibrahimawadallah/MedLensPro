## Analytics & Optimization

### Metrics to Track
- **Engagement rate:** Likes, comments, shares, saves
- **Follower growth:** Weekly and monthly
- **Click-through rate:** To website
- **Conversion rate:** Sign-ups, partnership inquiries
- **Community growth:** Discord members, Telegram subscribers

### Optimization Schedule
- **Weekly:** Review top-performing posts
- **Bi-weekly:** Adjust content strategy based on metrics
- **Monthly:** Comprehensive analytics review
- **Quarterly:** Strategy refresh and goal setting

### A/B Testing
- **Test different posting times**
- **Test different content formats**
- **Test different hashtags**
- **Test different call-to-actions**

## Crisis Communication

### Potential Issues
- **Technical outage:** Communicate transparently, provide updates
- **Negative feedback:** Respond professionally, address concerns
- **Misinformation:** Correct politely with sources
- **Partnership delays:** Manage expectations, communicate timeline

### Response Protocol
1. **Acknowledge:** Within 1 hour
2. **Investigate:** Gather facts
3. **Respond:** Within 24 hours with solution
4. **Follow up:** Ensure resolution
