## Phone/Video Call Scripts

### Script 1: Initial Discovery Call (Pharmacy Partnership)

**Introduction:**
"Hi [Name], thank you for taking the time to speak with me today. I really appreciate it. I know you're busy, so I'll keep this to 15 minutes as promised."

**Purpose:**
"As I mentioned in my email, I'm the founder of MedLens Pro. We're a bilingual drug information platform serving patients across the MENA region. Today, I'd love to learn more about [Pharmacy Name]'s current digital initiatives and explore potential partnership opportunities."

**Discovery Questions:**
1. "Can you tell me a bit about [Pharmacy Name]'s current mobile app or digital offerings?"
2. "What are your biggest priorities when it comes to patient engagement and education?"
3. "How do you currently handle patient questions about medications?"
4. "What challenges do you face in helping patients understand their medications?"
5. "Are there any digital initiatives you're currently planning or exploring?"

**Value Proposition:**
"Based on what you've shared, I think there could be a strong opportunity for us to work together. Our platform can help [address specific pain point they mentioned] by [specific benefit]."

**Partnership Models:**
"We're flexible in how we partner, but here are a few models that might work:
1. API integration for your app
2. Co-branded patient education materials
3. Affiliate partnership
4. Referral program

Which of these sounds most interesting to you, or would you like to explore a different approach?"

**Next Steps:**
"I'd love to send over a more detailed proposal tailored to [Pharmacy Name]'s needs. Would that be helpful? If so, I can have that to you by [date]. Then we could schedule a follow-up call to discuss details."

**Closing:**
"Thank you again for your time today. I really appreciate your insights and look forward to potentially working together."

### Script 2: Technical Discussion Call (API Integration)

**Introduction:**
"Hi [Name], thanks for joining. Today I want to walk you through our API capabilities and discuss how we might integrate with [Company Name]'s systems."

**Technical Overview:**
"Our API provides:
• Drug information lookup by name, NDC, or barcode
• Patient-friendly sections and summaries
• Bilingual content (English/Arabic)
• Drug interaction data
• Response times under 200ms
• 99.9% uptime SLA"

**Integration Options:**
"There are a few ways we could integrate:
1. **Direct API Integration:** Your app calls our API directly
2. **SDK Integration:** We provide a SDK for easier implementation
3. **Widget Integration:** Embeddable widget for your website
4. **White-label Solution:** Fully branded version of our platform

Which approach aligns best with your technical architecture?"

**Technical Requirements:**
"For integration, you'll need:
• API key (we'll provide)
• Basic authentication
• Rate limit handling (we can help with this)
• Error handling guidelines

We provide comprehensive documentation and can offer technical support during implementation."

**Timeline:**
"Typical integration timeline:
• Week 1: Technical review and planning
• Week 2-3: Development and testing
• Week 4: Launch and optimization

We can adjust this based on your resources and priorities."

**Next Steps:**
"I'll send over our technical documentation and API sandbox access. Would you like your technical team to review this before our next call?"

**Closing:**
"Great, I'll get that to you by [date]. Let's schedule a follow-up for [date/time] to discuss any technical questions and plan the integration."

### Script 3: Partnership Negotiation Call

**Introduction:**
"Hi [Name], thanks for joining. Today I want to discuss the partnership terms and make sure we're aligned on expectations."

**Value Exchange:**
"Let's recap what each side brings to this partnership:

**From MedLens Pro:**
• API access and technical support
• Co-branded materials
• Marketing support
• Revenue sharing (if applicable)

**From [Company Name]:**
• Integration into your platforms
• Marketing to your customer base
• Feedback on product development
• [Other commitments]"

**Terms Discussion:**
"Regarding the specific terms:
• **Revenue Share:** We propose [X%] split. How does that align with your expectations?
• **Exclusivity:** Are you looking for exclusive rights in your market/region?
• **Timeline:** When would you like to launch?
• **Support Level:** What level of ongoing support do you expect?"

**Concerns/Objections:**
"Do you have any concerns about the proposed terms? I want to make sure this works for both parties."

**Next Steps:**
"I'll draft a formal agreement based on our discussion today. Can you review it by [date]? Then we can schedule a final call to sign and plan the launch."

**Closing:**
"Excellent, I'm excited about this partnership and look forward to working together."

## Best Practices for Outreach

### Timing
- **Best days to send:** Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
- **Best times:** 10 AM - 2 PM local time
- **Avoid:** Mondays (busy), Fridays (weekend mode), holidays

### Personalization
- Always research the recipient before reaching out
- Mention specific achievements or projects
- Reference recent company news or initiatives
- Customize for their specific role and industry

### Follow-Up Strategy
- **First follow-up:** 3 days after initial email
- **Second follow-up:** 7 days after first follow-up
- **Third follow-up:** 14 days after second follow-up
- **Stop after:** 3 follow-ups or explicit rejection

### Value-First Approach
- Lead with value, not sales pitch
- Share relevant insights or data
- Offer free resources or trials
- Focus on solving their problems

### Professional Communication
- Keep emails concise (under 200 words)
- Use clear subject lines
- Proofread carefully
- Include clear call-to-action

### Relationship Building
- Focus on long-term relationships
- Don't be pushy or aggressive
- Respect their time and boundaries
- Add value even if they don't convert
