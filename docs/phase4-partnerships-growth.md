## Risk Mitigation

### Partnership Risks
- **Low Response Rate:** Mitigate with personalized outreach, follow-ups
- **Integration Complexity:** Start with simple integrations, iterate
- **Revenue Uncertainty:** Focus on value-first partnerships

### Community Risks
- **Low Engagement:** Seed with early adopters, create valuable content
- **Moderation Challenges:** Establish clear guidelines, active moderation
- **Platform Dependency:** Diversify across multiple platforms

### Content Risks
- **Rejection Rates:** Build relationships first, pitch second
- **Quality Concerns:** Maintain high editorial standards
- **Timeline Delays:** Have backup content ready
